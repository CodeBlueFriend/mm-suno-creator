# Songwriting and structure

## Structure first

Assign every section a function and time budget before drafting. For songs under three minutes, favor a short intro, an early first chorus, two compact verses, one bridge or contrast section, and a brief outro. Estimate rather than promise duration.

## Chinese lyrics

- Prefer phrases that can be articulated in one breath.
- Check crowded consonants, awkward proper nouns, and long strings of closed syllables.
- Use rhyme as support, not as a reason to flatten meaning.
- Flag important lexical tones that may fight sustained melody; do not pretend text alone determines the generated melody.

## English lyrics

- Put content words near likely stresses and use contractions when natural.
- Favor vowels that can sustain on hook notes.
- Avoid forcing filler words only to complete rhyme.

## Bilingual lyrics

Make both languages independently idiomatic and singable. Preserve scene, emotional turn, and hook function rather than literal wording.

## Anti-template review

Vary line length and syntax intentionally. Remove generic summaries, stacked abstractions, symmetrical verse padding, and metaphors that do not advance the scene. Keep one concrete image or action in each major section.
