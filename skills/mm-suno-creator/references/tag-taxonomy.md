# Tag taxonomy and evidence

Build Style prompts in this order:

1. one primary genre;
2. zero to two compatible secondary or era directions;
3. tempo/groove and energy;
4. vocal identity and delivery;
5. core instruments and arrangement;
6. production and space;
7. mood and exclusions.

Use tags only when they change an audible or structural decision. Remove synonyms and conflicting commands. A community-observed tag is not official platform documentation.

Evidence states in the bundled dictionary:

- `curated_music_term`: stable music meaning; platform execution is not guaranteed;
- `supported_observed`: repeatedly useful in internal/community observation, with a verification date;
- `experimental`: plausible but unstable; do not make it a high-weight default;
- `unknown`: preserve the query and request evidence rather than inventing a definition.
